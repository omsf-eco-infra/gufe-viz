**Added:**

* ``<gufe-view>``, the dispatcher: set its ``.payload`` and it mounts the view
  that claims the payload's ``kind``. Unknown kinds, missing kinds and payloads
  written by a newer gufe-viz are refused by name rather than thrown at the
  caller.
* ``<gufe-small-molecule>`` - 2D RDKit depiction beside a 3D 3Dmol conformer,
  with style switcher and a SMILES / charge / atom-count info bar.
* ``<gufe-protein>`` - 3Dmol with representation and colour-scheme switchers,
  water and hetero toggles, and chain / residue / atom statistics.
* A Vite dev app: drop a payload JSON anywhere on the page to render it, and a
  gallery page that renders every ``examples/*.json`` at once.
